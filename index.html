Research agenda

What we intend to find out

These are the questions we are starting with. They are not yet answered, and the agenda will change as we learn.

Sector structure, supply chains and operating economics.

Questions on our agenda

  1. How do staple-food supply chains in East Africa work, from farm to market?
  2. What are the operating economics of storage and logistics businesses?
  3. Which sectors turn local demand into durable enterprises?

Markets, capital and financial conditions.

Questions on our agenda

  1. What does the cost of capital look like for small and medium enterprises in Kenya?
  2. How do interest-rate cycles change the returns on patient capital?
  3. What makes capital allocation disciplined rather than merely cautious?

Growth, inflation, policy and the forces behind them.

Questions on our agenda

  1. What drives food and fuel prices in Kenya, and how fast do shocks pass through?
  2. How do currency moves reach households and firms?
  3. What does public borrowing do to private credit?

Kenya, East Africa and neighbouring markets.

Questions on our agenda

  1. Where do prices for the same commodity differ across East African markets, and why?
  2. What do cross-border trade rules change for producers?
  3. Which regional infrastructure projects change the economics of whole sectors?

Worldwide markets and the economies that move them.

Questions on our agenda

  1. How do global commodity and rate cycles reach African markets?
  2. What does the energy transition change for resource economies?
  3. Which global technology shifts matter for real industries?

Planned agenda. Subject to change. Answers appear in Publications as they are completed.

Method

How we hold ourselves to account

A new research house earns trust by being checkable.

Sources named

Every piece lists its sources and the date each was accessed.

Conclusions dated

We say what we concluded and when, so changes of mind are visible.

Confidence stated

High, moderate or low, defined on the method page.

Predictions graded

Correct, partly correct or incorrect, published openly.

Errors corrected

Openly, with a dated note.

Interests disclosed

Where BlackVault or a venture has a stake, the piece says so.
